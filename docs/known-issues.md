# Known Issues and Findings Register

Status: **Proposed — awaiting approval**
Last updated: 2026-11-20
Sources:

- **CRM audit**: read-only audit of `bpc_crm/frontend` (local clone, branch `dev` @ `0d155d455`, 58+ commits behind `origin/dev`).
- **Figma audit**: read-only analysis of _BPC CRM – DS V2.0.0_ (`Vg3xTJzSC89ASDPlkDiu8t`) and _BPC Technician App (Copy)_ (`eQsrTzf059xBW4h76G9zUY`).

This is the single register of findings. The other documents refer to findings by ID.

## Priority levels

| Level  | Meaning                                                         |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **P0** | Must resolve before the first local prototype runs.             |
| **P1** | Must resolve before the first demo.                             |
| **P2** | Can be resolved while later screens are built.                  |
| **P3** | Future production considerations; not needed for the prototype. |

"Approval" means a person (product owner or designer) has to sign off on the recommended action. "Blocks" means the prototype can't proceed until the item is resolved.
